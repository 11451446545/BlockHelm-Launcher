# 26A17091

* Refine the liquid-glass material on compact buttons with high-transparency Gaussian sampling, restrained edge refraction, and pointer-local highlights.
* Prevent hover refraction from saturating the material or producing large cyan/blue light patches at the edge.
* Keep the material consistent across light and dark themes while preserving a stable Gaussian and edge-highlight fallback.
* Improve rounded-edge pointer tracking with interruptible motion and stop the render loop once the pointer settles.
